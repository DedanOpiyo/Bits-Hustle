## 1. Welcome to Bits Hustle Hub

Bits Hustle Hub (B2H) is the central platform for managing campus-based brand ambassadors, tracking activations, capturing leads, managing campaigns, and rewarding ambassadors for their work.

### What You Can Do on This Platform

| Role | Key Capabilities |
|------|------------------|
| **Ambassador** | Check in at hubs, complete tasks, capture leads, share referrals, track earnings, view leaderboards, access training |
| **Hub Manager** | Monitor attendance, approve/reject task submissions, post announcements, view hub performance |
| **Super Admin** | Full system management — users, hubs, campaigns, reports, content, settings |
| **Finance Officer** | Review wallets, approve/reject payouts, export finance records, audit commissions |
| **Support Officer** | Manage support tickets, escalate issues, respond to ambassador inquiries |
| **Partner/Advertiser** | Create campaigns, view campaign performance, access reports |

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating Your Account

1. Navigate to the Bits Hustle Hub login page
2. Click **"Register"** or use an invitation link
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Password
   - Select your campus/hub (if prompted)
4. Complete your profile including your referral code (auto-generated)
5. Log in with your email and password

### 2.2 First Login

After your first login, you will:
- See your **Dashboard** with personalized stats
- Be prompted to **change your password** if this is a temporary password
- See available **announcements** from your hub or system admin
- Have access to your **wallet** (ambassadors only)

## 5. Ambassador User Guide

### 5.1 Profile & Referral Code

Your ambassador profile includes:
- **Personal Information**: Name, email, phone, campus, hub assignment
- **Referral Code**: Unique code automatically assigned to you (e.g., `B2H-ABC123`)
- **Tier**: Junior Ambassador → Ambassador → Senior Ambassador → Campus Lead → Hub Officer
- **Badges**: Earned achievements displayed on your profile
- **Status**: Active, Inactive, or Suspended

**How to view your profile:**
1. Click your avatar/name in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown

### 5.2 Checking In & Out at Your Hub

**Purpose:** Mark your attendance at your campus hub to track your working hours and qualify for attendance-based rewards.

**How to check in:**
1. Navigate to **QR Check-In** from the navigation bar
2. Scan the QR code displayed at your hub OR enter the hub manually
3. Click **"Check In"**
4. You will see a confirmation with your check-in time

**How to check out:**
1. Go to the same **QR Check-In** page
2. Click **"Check Out"** when leaving the hub
3. Your session duration will be calculated

**Important Rules:**
- Only **one check-in per day** per hub (unless manager override approved)
- Always **check out** to properly track your session duration
- Your check-in history is viewable on your dashboard

### 5.3 Viewing & Completing Tasks

**Purpose:** Tasks are specific actions you perform to achieve campaign objectives. Completing tasks earns you rewards.

**How to view tasks:**
1. Click **Tasks** in the navigation bar
2. You'll see tasks organized by:
   - **Available** — Ready to work on
   - **In Progress** — Started but not submitted
   - **Submitted** — Waiting for review
   - **Approved** — Completed and rewarded
   - **Rejected** — Needs resubmission or appeal

**How to submit a task:**
1. Find a task you want to work on and click **"Submit Proof"**
2. Fill in the submission form:
   - **Description**: Describe what you did
   - **Proof**: Upload a photo, provide a link, or attach a document (required)
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Your submission will be reviewed by your Hub Manager
5. If **approved** → Reward is credited to your wallet
6. If **rejected** → You'll see the reason and can resubmit or appeal

**Important Rules:**
- Only **approved** submissions earn rewards
- You **cannot approve your own tasks**
- Submissions after the deadline may be rejected
- You can **appeal a rejection once** — the appeal decision is final

### 5.4 Capturing Leads

**Purpose:** Leads are potential customers whose contact information you submit for Bits platforms.

**How to capture a lead:**
1. Click **Leads** in the navigation bar
2. Click **"Capture Lead"** or **"Add Lead"**
3. Fill in the lead form:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email Address**
   - **Phone Number**
   - **Bits Platform** — Select which platform the lead is interested in (see [Section 14](#14-bits-platforms-reference) for all platforms)
   - **Notes** (optional) — Any additional context
4. Click **"Submit"**

**Important Rules:**
- Each lead is tagged to **exactly one** Bits platform
- **Duplicate detection** is automatic — same phone or email in the same campaign will be flagged
- **First ambassador to submit** a lead owns it
- Lead statuses: New → Contacted → Converted → Lost
- Duplicate leads do **not** generate earnings

### 5.5 Sharing Referrals

**Purpose:** Referrals bring new users into the Bits ecosystem. You earn commissions when your referrals sign up and convert.

**How to share your referral code:**
1. Go to **Referrals** in the navigation bar
2. Copy your unique referral code (e.g., `B2H-ABC123`)
3. Share it via:
   - Direct message
   - Social media
   - Email
   - QR code
   - Referral link

**How to track referrals:**
1. Go to **Referrals**
2. View your referral list showing:
   - Who signed up using your code
   - Which platform they joined
   - Conversion status (New, Contacted, Converted, Lost)
   - Earnings generated

**Important Rules:**
- One referral code works across **all 10 Bits platforms**
- The system tracks which platform each referred person signed up for
- Referral credit is valid for **30 days** after signup (attribution window)
- Conversions after the attribution window may not earn commission

### 5.6 Wallet & Earnings

**Purpose:** Track all your earnings, view transaction history, and request payouts.

**How to view your wallet:**
1. Click **Payouts** or your wallet balance on the dashboard
2. You'll see:
   - **Current Balance** — Amount available for withdrawal
   - **Pending Earnings** — Earnings from approved tasks not yet paid
   - **Total Earned** — Lifetime earnings
   - **Total Withdrawn** — Lifetime withdrawals
   - **Transaction History** — Line-by-line record of every earning and payout

**Earning Sources:**
| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Approved Tasks | Fixed reward per completed task |
| Leads Submitted | Reward per valid lead |
| Referrals Converted | Reward when referred person signs up and converts |
| Campaign Bonuses | Extra rewards for hitting campaign targets |
| Attendance | Rewards for consistent check-ins |
| Leaderboard Bonuses | Periodic rewards for top performers |

### 5.7 Requesting a Payout

**Purpose:** Withdraw your earned commissions to your mobile money or bank account.

**How to request a payout:**
1. Go to **Payouts**
2. Click **"Request Payout"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Amount** — Must meet the minimum threshold (e.g., KES 500)
   - **Payment Method** — M-Pesa, Bank Transfer, etc.
   - **Account Details** — Phone number or bank account
4. Click **"Submit Request"**
5. Your request will be reviewed by a Finance Officer
6. If **approved** → Payment is processed, wallet is debited
7. If **rejected** → You'll be notified with a reason

**Important Rules:**
- **Minimum payout threshold** applies (e.g., KES 500)
- All requests require **Finance Officer approval**
- If rejected, you can **appeal once** with a reason
- Every transaction is logged in your **audit trail**

### 5.8 Leaderboards

**Purpose:** See how you rank against other ambassadors and earn recognition.

**How to view leaderboards:**
1. Click **Leaderboard** in the navigation bar
2. Switch between tabs:
   - **Hub Ranking** — Within your campus
   - **National Ranking** — Across all hubs
   - **Campaign Ranking** — Within a specific campaign

**Ranking Factors:**
- Total points earned
- Total leads captured
- Total referrals converted
- Tasks completed
- Earnings generated

### 5.9 Badges & Achievements

**Purpose:** Earn badges for milestones and showcase your achievements.

**Available Badges:**
| Badge | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Lead Hunter | Captured 50+ leads |
| Campus Closer | Converted 20+ leads to sales |
| Top Recruiter | Brought in 30+ referrals |
| Digital Hustler | Completed 100+ tasks |
| Hub Champion | Ranked #1 in hub for the month |
| Conversion King/Queen | Highest conversion rate in campaign |

**How to view your badges:**
1. Go to **Leaderboard** or your **Profile**
2. Click **"My Badges"** to see all earned badges

### 5.10 Ambassador Tiers (Growth Path)

| Tier | Criteria | Benefits |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Junior Ambassador** | Newly onboarded | Base commission rate |
| **Ambassador** | Completed 20 tasks | Slightly higher commission |

### 5.11 Learning Hub

**Purpose:** Access training materials to improve your skills and performance.

**How to access learning resources:**
1. Click **Learning Hub** in the navigation bar
2. Browse resources by category:

### 5.12 Notifications

**Purpose:** Stay informed about tasks, earnings, payouts, and announcements.

**When You Get Notified:**
| Event | Channel |
|-------|---------|

| Task deadline approaching | In-app |

**Purpose:** Read important notices from your hub manager and system administrators.

**How to view announcements:**

## 6. Hub Manager User Guide

### 6.1 Monitoring Attendance

**Purpose:** See which ambassadors checked in to your hub today and track attendance patterns.

**How to view attendance:**
1. Go to your **Dashboard** or **Hub Management**
2. View the **"Today's Attendance"** section
3. See a list of ambassadors with:
   - Check-in time
   - Check-out time (if checked out)
   - Session duration
4. Filter by date range to view historical attendance

### 6.2 Reviewing & Approving Task Submissions

**Purpose:** Review task submissions from ambassadors in your hub and approve or reject them.

**How to review submissions:**
1. Go to **Tasks** and look for the **"Pending Review"** tab
2. Alternatively, click the notification about pending submissions
3. For each submission:
   - View the ambassador's name
   - Read the task description
   - Review the submitted proof (photo, link, document)
4. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"**
   - **If approving**: Reward is automatically credited to the ambassador's wallet
   - **If rejecting**: You **must provide a reason** — the ambassador will see it

**Important Rules:**
- You **cannot approve your own tasks** (if you also submit)
- Approvals are **final** unless the ambassador appeals
- All approvals/rejections are logged in the **audit trail**

### 6.3 Posting Hub Announcements

**Purpose:** Communicate important information to ambassadors in your hub.

**How to post an announcement:**
1. Go to **Announcements**
2. Click **"Post Announcement"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — Clear, concise headline
   - **Content** — Full message
   - **Priority** — Low, Medium, High, Urgent
   - **Expiration Date** (optional) — When the announcement should be removed
4. Click **"Publish"**
5. The announcement will appear on all ambassadors' dashboards in your hub

### 6.4 Viewing Hub Performance

**Purpose:** Monitor your hub's overall performance and identify areas for improvement.

**How to view hub analytics:**
1. Go to **Analytics** → **Hub KPIs**
2. View metrics including:
   - Daily attendance trends
   - Active ambassadors per hub
   - Leads generated by hub
   - Conversions by hub
   - Total earnings distributed
3. Compare your hub against other hubs nationally

### 6.5 Managing Ambassadors in Your Hub

**Purpose:** Supervise ambassadors assigned to your hub.

**What you can do:**
- View ambassador profiles and performance
- Check individual ambassador stats (tasks, leads, referrals, earnings)
- View an ambassador's wallet balance (read-only)
- Flag suspicious activity for fraud review

## 7. Super Admin User Guide

### 7.1 User Management

**Purpose:** Create, manage, suspend, or deactivate users across the system.

**How to manage users:**
1. Go to **User Management**
2. View all users with filters by role, status, hub
3. **Create a new user:**
   - Click **"Add User"**
   - Fill in: email, name, phone, role, hub assignment
   - Click **"Create"**
   - User receives a temporary password
4. **Edit a user:** Click the edit icon on any user row
5. **Activate/Deactivate:** Toggle the user's active status
6. **Delete:** Remove a user (requires confirmation)

### 7.2 Hub Management

**Purpose:** Create, edit, and manage campus hubs across the system.

**How to manage hubs:**
1. Go to **Hub Management**
2. View all hubs with their assigned managers
3. **Create a new hub:**
   - Click **"Add Hub"**
   - Fill in: hub name, location, assign a hub manager
   - Click **"Create"**
4. **Edit a hub:** Update name, location, or reassign the manager
5. **Delete a hub:** Remove a hub (requires confirmation — note: ambassadors may be affected)

### 7.3 Campaign Management

**Purpose:** Create, manage, and monitor marketing campaigns.

**How to create a campaign:**
1. Go to **Campaigns** (via Tasks or Partner Portal)
2. Click **"Create Campaign"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Campaign Name**
   - **Description**
   - **Bits Platform** — Select from the official list (see [Section 14](#14-bits-platforms-reference))
   - **Objective** — Signups, Downloads, Leads, Sales, Traffic, Awareness
   - **Start Date** and **End Date**
   - **Budget** (optional)
   - **Reward Structure** — How ambassadors will be rewarded
4. Click **"Create"**
5. Assign tasks to the campaign

**How to manage campaigns:**
- **Edit** campaign details (name, dates, budget)
- **Review/Approve** campaigns submitted by partners
- **Deactivate** campaigns that are no longer active
- **View performance** — leads generated, conversions, ROI

### 7.4 Task Management

**Purpose:** Create and assign tasks within campaigns.

**How to create a task:**
1. Go to **Tasks**
2. Click **"Create Task"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Task Type** — Lead Capture, Referral Drive, Event Promotion, Product Demo, Social Media Share, Customer Onboarding, Flyer Distribution, Survey Collection
   - **Description** — Clear instructions for ambassadors
   - **Reward Amount** — KES earned per approved submission
   - **Deadline** — When the task must be completed
   - **Bits Platform** — Associated platform
   - **Evidence Required** — Photo, link, or document upload
4. Assign the task to specific ambassadors or to all ambassadors in a hub
5. Click **"Create"**

### 7.5 System-Wide Announcements

**Purpose:** Broadcast important messages to all users across all hubs.

**How to post:**
1. Go to **Announcements**
2. Click **"Post Announcement"**
3. Select **"System-Wide"** as the scope
4. Fill in title, content, priority, and expiration
5. Click **"Publish"**

### 7.6 Viewing All Reports

**Purpose:** Access system-wide analytics and export data.

**Available Reports:**
| Report | What It Shows |
|--------|--------------|
| **Ambassador KPIs** | Total/active ambassadors, retention, top performers, average earnings |
| **Hub KPIs** | Daily attendance, active ambassadors per hub, top performing hubs |
| **Campaign KPIs** | Tasks completed, leads generated, conversions, conversion rate, CPA, ROI |
| **Finance KPIs** | Total commissions, payouts approved/pending, turnaround time |
| **Platform KPIs** | Leads per platform, conversions per platform, commissions per platform |
| **Seven Daily Questions** | Who showed up, who worked, what they produced, revenue, payments, winning hubs, leaks |
| **Funnel Analysis** | Conversion rates at each stage, drop-off points |

**Exporting Reports:**
1. Go to the report you want to export
2. Click **"Export"**
3. Choose format: **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**
4. The file will download to your computer

### 7.7 Audit Logs

**Purpose:** View a complete record of all system changes for compliance and security.

**How to view audit logs:**
1. Go to **Audit Logs** (Super Admin only)
2. Filter by:
   - User
   - Action type
   - Entity type (users, campaigns, payouts, etc.)
   - Date range
3. Each log entry shows:
   - Who made the change
   - What was changed
   - Before and after values
   - Timestamp and IP address

### 7.8 Fraud Management

**Purpose:** Monitor and resolve fraud alerts across the system.

**How to manage fraud:**
1. Go to **Fraud Alerts**
2. View all active alerts with severity levels
3. For each alert:
   - Review the details (type, ambassador, evidence)
   - Take action: Warning, Suspension, or Permanent Ban
4. **Fraud Penalty Matrix:**
   | Violation | Consequence |
   |-----------|-------------|
   | Fake lead submission | Warning + lead rejected |
   | Repeated fake leads | Suspension + clawback of related earnings |
   | Duplicate referral fraud | Permanent ban |
   | Identity fraud | Permanent ban + legal escalation |

### 7.9 Platform & Integration Management

**Purpose:** Manage Bits platforms, API keys, and external integrations.

**How to manage:**
1. Go to **Platform Integrations**
2. View all registered Bits platforms
3. Manage API keys for external platform integrations
4. Configure webhook endpoints for external conversion tracking
5. View webhook metrics and health status

### 7.10 Type Management

**Purpose:** Manage system enums and lookup types (roles, tiers, task types, action types).

**How to manage:**
1. Go to **Manage Types**
2. Manage:
   - **Role Types** — User roles in the system
   - **Tier Types** — Ambassador tiers and criteria
   - **Task Type Types** — Task categorization
   - **Action Types** — Platform action types
   - **Lead Status Types** — Lead lifecycle statuses
   - **Task Status Types** — Task submission statuses
   - **Payout Status Types** — Payout request statuses

## 8. Finance Officer User Guide

### 8.1 Reviewing Wallet Transactions

**Purpose:** Audit ambassador wallets and transaction records.

**How to review:**
1. Go to **Ambassadors** or **Wallets**
2. Select an ambassador to view their wallet details
3. Review:
   - Current balance
   - Pending earnings
   - Total earned
   - Total withdrawn
   - Full transaction history with source references
4. All transactions are tied to verifiable source records (task ID, lead ID, referral ID)

### 8.2 Approving & Rejecting Payouts

**Purpose:** Review payout requests and approve or reject them.

**How to process payouts:**
1. Go to **Payouts**
2. View the **"Pending"** tab for all pending requests
3. For each request:
   - Review the ambassador's details
   - Verify the requested amount against their wallet balance
   - Check for any fraud flags
4. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"**
   - **If approving**: Payment is processed (M-Pesa or bank transfer), wallet is debited
   - **If rejecting**: Provide a reason — the ambassador will be notified

### 8.3 Exporting Finance Records

**Purpose:** Export payout and commission records for accounting and auditing.

**How to export:**
1. Go to **Analytics** → **Finance KPIs**
2. Click **"Export"**
3. Choose format: **CSV**, **Excel**, or **PDF**
4. Available data:
   - Total commissions earned
   - Total payouts approved
   - Pending payouts
   - Payout turnaround time
   - Average payout amount
   - Per-platform commission breakdown

### 8.4 Monitoring Commission Obligations

**Purpose:** Track total commission liability across all ambassadors.

**How to monitor:**
1. Go to **Analytics** → **Finance KPIs**
2. View:
   - Current total commission liability (pending payouts)
   - Commission trends over time
   - Commission by platform
   - Commission by hub

## 9. Support Officer User Guide

### 9.1 Viewing & Responding to Tickets

**Purpose:** Manage support tickets submitted by ambassadors.

**How to manage tickets:**
1. Go to **Support Tickets**
2. View all open tickets organized by:
   - Category (Account issues, Task disputes, Missing earnings, Payout problems, Technical issues, General inquiry)
   - Priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
   - Status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
3. Click on a ticket to view details
4. **Respond** to the ticket with your reply
5. **Update status** as you work through the ticket

### 9.2 Escalating Issues

**Purpose:** Escalate unresolved issues to higher-level support.

**Escalation Process:**
1. Review the ticket and attempt to resolve it
2. If unresolved within 48 hours, **escalate to Hub Manager**
3. If still unresolved, **escalate to Super Admin**
4. The final decision is documented in the **audit log**

### 9.3 Ticket Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Account Issues | Login problems, profile updates, account suspension |
| Task Approval Disputes | Disagreement with task rejection |
| Missing Earnings | Earnings not reflected in wallet |
| Payout Problems | Payout not received, wrong amount |
| Technical Issues | Bugs, errors, system malfunctions |
| General Inquiry | Questions, feedback, suggestions |

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Go to the login page, click "Forgot Password," enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can I change my role?**  
A: No. Roles are assigned by Super Admins. Contact your admin if you need a role change.

**Q: How many hubs can I be assigned to?**  
A: Ambassadors are typically assigned to one primary hub. Hub Managers can manage one hub at a time.

### Tasks & Earnings

**Q: When do I get paid for a completed task?**  
A: After your Hub Manager approves your submission, the reward is credited to your wallet immediately.

**Q: What happens if my task submission is rejected?**  
A: You'll see the rejection reason. You can resubmit with corrected proof or file one appeal.

**Q: Can I appeal a payout rejection?**  
A: Yes, you can appeal once. Go to Payouts, find the rejected request, and click "Appeal."

### Leads & Referrals

**Q: What happens if I submit a duplicate lead?**  
A: The system will flag it as a duplicate. Duplicate leads do not earn commissions.

**Q: How long is my referral code valid?**  
A: Your referral code works indefinitely, but referral credit (commission) is valid for 30 days after the referred person signs up.

**Q: Does my referral code work for all Bits platforms?**  
A: Yes! One referral code works across all 10 Bits platforms. The system tracks which platform each referred person joined.

### Wallet & Payouts

**Q: What is the minimum payout amount?**  
A: The minimum payout threshold is KES 500 (may vary during pilot).

**Q: How long does a payout take?**  
A: After approval by Finance, payouts are processed via M-Pesa or bank transfer. Processing time depends on your Finance team's turnaround.

**Q: Can I see which platform my earnings came from?**  
A: Yes. Go to your wallet and view the transaction history — each transaction shows the associated Bits platform.

### Attendance

**Q: Can I check in to multiple hubs in one day?**  
A: No. Only one check-in per day per hub, unless your Hub Manager approves an override.

**Q: What if I forget to check out?**  
A: Your check-out time will be recorded when you next check in, or your Hub Manager can manually close the session.

### Fraud & Compliance

**Q: What happens if I submit fake leads?**  
A: First offense: warning + lead rejected. Repeated offenses: suspension + earnings clawback. Severe fraud: permanent ban.

**Q: How is duplicate lead detection handled?**  
A: The system automatically checks for matching phone numbers or email addresses within the same campaign.

### Inactivity

**Q: What happens if I'm inactive for a long time?**  
A: After 30 days without check-in or task submission, your account will be marked inactive. Reactivation requires manager approval or new orientation.

## 13. Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Invalid email or password" | Check your credentials. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. |
| Account locked/suspended | Contact your Hub Manager or Super Admin. |
| "Must change password" | This happens with temporary passwords. Follow the prompt to set a new password. |

### Task Submission Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't submit proof | Check that you have a file to upload or a valid link. Ensure the task is still active. |
| Submission not showing | Wait a moment and refresh. If still missing, contact support. |
| Task deadline passed | Late submissions may be rejected. Contact your Hub Manager for an exception. |

### Wallet & Payout Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Earnings not reflected | Approved earnings appear immediately. Check your transaction history. If missing, contact Finance. |
| Payout request rejected | Check the rejection reason. You can appeal once. |
| Payout not received | Processing may take 1-3 business days. Contact Finance if it exceeds this. |

### Lead Capture Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Duplicate lead" error | This lead (same phone/email) was already submitted for this campaign. |
| Can't capture a lead | Ensure all required fields are filled. Check that the platform is selected. |

### Check-In Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| QR code not scanning | Ensure your camera has permission. Try manual hub selection. |
| "Already checked in today" | You can only check in once per day. Contact your Hub Manager for an override. |
| Can't check out | Ensure you have an active check-in session. Contact support if the issue persists. |
