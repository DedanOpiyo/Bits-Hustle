Bits Hustle Hub (B2H)
Business Requirements Document (BRD)
Version 1.0
1. Document Purpose
This document defines what the Bits Hustle Hub platform must do from a business perspective. It is intended for:

Founders and decision-makers

Product managers

Investors and stakeholders

Anyone who needs to understand the business logic before development begins

Technical implementation details are intentionally excluded. For developers, refer to the separate Technical Implementation Guide.

2. Product Overview
2.1 Product Name
Bits Hustle Hub (B2H)

2.2 Product Type
A campus-based digital activation, ambassador management, lead generation, and youth earning platform.

2.3 Product Summary
Bits Hustle Hub is a platform designed to recruit, manage, and empower students and youth as brand ambassadors across campuses and physical activation hubs.

The platform enables ambassadors to:

Register and onboard

Check in at physical hubs

Access tasks and campaigns

Market Bits platforms and products

Capture leads and referrals

Earn commissions and incentives

Access learning materials

Compete on leaderboards

Grow into long-term ecosystem contributors

The platform enables Bits management to:

Manage hubs and ambassadors

Run and monitor campaigns

Track leads, signups, and conversions

Approve tasks and commissions

Monitor hub performance

Measure ROI and growth

3. Product Vision
3.1 Vision Statement
To build a scalable youth-powered activation and earning ecosystem that transforms campuses into productive digital growth engines for Bits platforms and partner brands.

3.2 Core Promise
Every day, the platform answers seven critical business questions:

#	Question
1	Who showed up?
2	Who worked?
3	What did they produce?
4	What growth or revenue came from it?
5	What should they be paid?
6	Which hubs are winning?
7	Where are we leaking performance?
If the platform answers these clearly, it is doing its job.

4. Problem Statement
Bits needs a structured digital system to coordinate a large network of student ambassadors and physical hubs.

Without a platform, Bits struggles to:

Manage ambassador recruitment at scale

Assign and track marketing tasks

Monitor hub attendance and usage

Validate lead generation and referrals

Calculate commissions fairly

Reduce fraud and duplicate submissions

Measure campaign and hub performance

This platform solves these operational and growth challenges.

5. Business Objectives
The platform helps Bits to:

Grow signups and customer acquisition across all Bits platforms

Create a campus-based ambassador ecosystem

Track and reward student marketing performance

Manage hub operations centrally

Reduce manual coordination and fraud

Scale to multiple campuses and eventually multiple countries

8. Campaigns & Tasks
8.1 What is a Campaign?
A campaign is a business objective with a start date, end date, budget, and reward structure.

Examples of campaign objectives:

Signups

Downloads

Leads

Sales

Traffic

Awareness

8.2 What is a Task?
A task is a specific action an ambassador performs to achieve campaign objectives.

Examples of tasks:

Task Type	Description
Lead Capture	Collect names, emails, and phone numbers of potential customers
Referral Drive	Share referral codes to bring in new signups
Event Promotion	Invite peers to Bits events or demos
Product Demo	Show classmates how to use Bits platforms
Social Media Share	Post campaign content online
Customer Onboarding	Help new users register and start using services
Flyer Distribution	Physically distribute marketing materials
Survey Collection	Gather feedback from peers
8.3 Task Workflow
Admin creates campaign and tasks

Tasks are assigned to ambassadors or hubs

Ambassador views available tasks

Ambassador completes task and submits proof

Hub Manager reviews submission

If approved → rewards are credited

If rejected → ambassador can appeal or resubmit

Rule: Only approved tasks earn rewards. No exceptions.

10. Earnings & Rewards
10.1 How Ambassadors Earn
Source	Description
Approved tasks	Fixed reward per completed task
Leads submitted	Reward per valid lead (may vary by quality)
Referrals converted	Reward when referred person signs up and converts
Campaign bonuses	Extra rewards for hitting campaign targets
Attendance	Potential rewards for consistent check-ins
Leaderboard bonuses	Periodic rewards for top performers
10.2 Wallet Structure
Every ambassador has a wallet that tracks:

Field	Description
Current balance	Amount available for withdrawal
Pending earnings	Earnings from approved tasks not yet paid
Total earned	Lifetime earnings
Total withdrawn	Lifetime withdrawals
Transaction history	Line-by-line record of every earning and payout
10.3 Payout Process
Ambassador requests payout (must meet minimum threshold)

Finance Officer reviews request

If approved → payment is processed, wallet is debited

If rejected → ambassador is notified with reason

Minimum payout threshold: To be defined during pilot (e.g., KES 500)

10.4 Transaction Integrity Rule
All earnings must be tied to a verifiable source record (task submission ID, lead ID, referral ID). No manual balance editing without audit trail.

12. Business Rules
These rules must be enforced by the platform.

12.1 Lead Rules
Rule	Description
Duplicate detection	Same phone or email in same campaign → flagged as duplicate
No double commissions	Duplicate leads do not generate earnings
Lead ownership	First ambassador to submit a lead owns it
Conversion tracking	Lead status can be updated (new, contacted, converted, lost)
12.2 Task Rules
Rule	Description
Approval required	Only approved submissions earn rewards
No self-approval	Ambassadors cannot approve their own tasks
Deadline enforcement	Submissions after deadline may be rejected
Evidence required	Tasks may require photo, link, or document upload
12.3 Attendance Rules
Rule	Description
One check-in per day	Per hub, unless manager override is approved
Check-out required	To properly track duration
Verification	Manager may verify attendance
12.4 Wallet & Payout Rules
Rule	Description
Transaction-based	Every earning has a source record
Minimum threshold	Payout requests below minimum are not allowed
Approval required	Finance must approve before payment
Audit trail	All changes logged
12.5 Permission Rules
Role	Cannot Do
Ambassador	Create campaigns, approve tasks, alter balances, approve payouts
Hub Manager	Create system-wide campaigns, approve payouts
Finance Officer	Create campaigns, manage hubs
12.6 Inactivity Policy (To be defined)
After X days without check-in or task submission → ambassador marked inactive

Reactivation requires manager approval or new orientation

12.7 Referral Attribution Period (To be defined)
Referral credit valid for X days after signup

Conversion after that period may not earn commission

12.8 Fraud Consequences
Violation	Consequence
Fake lead submission	Warning + lead rejected
Repeated fake leads	Suspension + clawback of related earnings
Duplicate referral fraud	Permanent ban
Identity fraud	Permanent ban + legal escalation

Goal: Scale nationally and regionally.
